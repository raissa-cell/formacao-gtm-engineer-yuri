# Quality Criteria: Proposal Follow-Up Email

## Scoring Rubric (use to self-evaluate output)

| Dimension | Weight | What "Good" Looks Like |
|-----------|--------|------------------------|
| **Personalization** | 30% | References specific pain, moment from call, or their words |
| **Clarity of CTA** | 25% | One action, one deadline, no ambiguity |
| **Brevity** | 20% | ≤280 words, no filler, every sentence earns its place |
| **Pain→Solution bridge** | 15% | Connects lead's stated problem to specific proposal element |
| **Subject line** | 10% | Specific, non-generic, curiosity or relevance trigger |

Score each 1–5. Target: ≥18/25 to send. Flag anything scoring ≤2 in any dimension.

---

## Anti-Patterns to Eliminate

### Opening killers
- ❌ "Espero que esteja bem / tudo bem com você"
- ❌ "Conforme conversamos anteriormente"
- ❌ "É com prazer que envio nossa proposta"
- ❌ "Segue em anexo a proposta solicitada"

### CTA failures
- ❌ "Fico à disposição para dúvidas" → too passive
- ❌ "Quando puder, dê uma olhada" → no urgency
- ❌ Two CTAs in one email → kills conversion

### Subject line fails
- ❌ "Proposta [Empresa]"
- ❌ "Follow-up da reunião"
- ❌ "Nossa solução para você"

### Structure failures
- ❌ Dumping the entire proposal into the email body
- ❌ Generic bullets not connected to the lead's pain
- ❌ Defensive tone when addressing objections

## High-Converting Openers (PT-BR examples)

Use as inspiration, not copy-paste:

- `"Durante nossa conversa, você mencionou que [specific pain] — é exatamente isso que [Solution X] resolve."`
- `"[Lead], ficou claro na reunião que o gargalo está em [specific stage]. A proposta que preparei ataca esse ponto diretamente."`
- `"Saí da nossa call com uma percepção clara: [Company] está perdendo [metric/opportunity] por [root cause]. Aqui está como resolvemos isso."`

## Objection Handling Patterns

| Objection Raised | Reframe in Email |
|-----------------|------------------|
| "Preciso apresentar internamente" | Offer a one-pager summary / offer to present together |
| "Está caro" | ROI anchor + what they're losing without solving it |
| "Já temos uma solução" | Acknowledge + differentiate on the specific gap they mentioned |
| "Não é o momento" | Create urgency around their stated deadline or cost of delay |
| "Preciso pensar" | Reduce friction — propose a smaller next step (30-min Q&A) |

## Benchmark: Nuvia/Datasaga Context

When writing for o usuário's companies, default to:
- **Sender persona**: COO / Partner / AI Specialist (depending on context)
- **Solution framing**: Revenue OS, ISA agent, outbound automation, AI qualification
- **Tone**: Smart, direct, no hype — peer-to-peer exec tone
- **Key metrics to reference if in transcript**: response rates, qualification speed, pipeline volume
- **Common objections**: "já temos SDRs", "nosso volume é pequeno", "não sei se funciona no nosso setor"
