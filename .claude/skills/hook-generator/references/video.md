# Surface: Video (Short + Long)

## Cut point (D1 Scroll-Stop)
The first 1–3 seconds of playback, with zero context and sound possibly off. If the
opening line does not create tension before the thumb keeps scrolling, the hook fails.

## Weights for scoring (restated from SKILL.md)
- Short:  D1 0.40 · D2 0.30 · D3 0.20 · D4 0.10
- Long:   D1 0.35 · D2 0.35 · D3 0.15 · D4 0.15
Confirm short vs long before scoring — the weight row differs.

## Length / structure
- Short-form: 5–12 words. Must survive a 1-second scroll. One breath, max.
- Long-form: 8–20 words. Slightly more setup allowed, still no warm-up.
- Hard rule: if it takes more than one breath to say, it is too long.
- Written as spoken VO. Em-dash ban does not apply (this is speech, not text).

## Types that over-index
Arresting Claim, Contrarian/Myth-Bust, Exact Pain, Identity Threat.
Statistic and Secret/Reveal work when the number/secret is genuinely surprising.

## Worked examples
Topic: founders ignoring GTM. Surface: Video — Short.
"You built a great product. Nobody's buying. Here's why."
D1 9 · D2 8 · D3 9 · D4 6 → 8.4/10. Cold-scroll stop + named pain + zero warm-up.

"Most founders confuse 'building' with 'selling.'"
D1 8 · D2 7 · D3 9 · D4 7 → 7.8/10. Contrarian, tight, mild share pull.
