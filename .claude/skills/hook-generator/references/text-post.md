# Surface: Text post (Instagram caption, Threads, Facebook, generic feed)

ALSO read `references/text-style-rules.md` before generating.

## Cut point (D1 Fold Survival)
The first line (and on IG, the first ~125 chars before "more") is the fold. Same logic
as LinkedIn but the audience is broader and less professional. Punch must land in line 1.

## Weights for scoring
D1 0.35 · D2 0.30 · D3 0.15 · D4 0.20

## Length / structure
- Line 1 = standalone statement that survives the fold.
- Optional rehook line 2.
- Threads: up to 500 chars, conversational register (closer to LinkedIn).
- IG caption: front-load the hook, the rest can run long.

## Types that over-index
Relatable Frustration / Exact Pain, Personal Story, Question, Contrast/Before-After.
(Broader audience → emotional and relatable beats niche-technical.)

## Worked examples
Topic: burnout in founders. Surface: Text post.
"I hit my revenue goal and felt nothing."
D1 9 · D2 9 · D3 9 · D4 7 → 8.6/10. Personal + unexpected emotional gap, fully in fold.

"Rest is not a reward you earn. It's the input."
D1 8 · D2 7 · D3 9 · D4 8 → 7.85/10. Contrarian reframe, shareable one-liner.
