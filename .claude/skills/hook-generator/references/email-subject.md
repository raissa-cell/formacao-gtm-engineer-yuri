# Surface: Email Subject Line

ALSO read `references/text-style-rules.md` before generating.

## Cut point (D1 Fold Survival)
The subject competes against the sender name and preview text in the inbox. Mobile
clients truncate around **35–45 characters**. The hook of the subject must land inside
~40 chars. Everything past that may never be seen.

## Weights for scoring
D1 0.40 · D2 0.40 · D3 0.15 · D4 0.05
(Fold and curiosity dominate equally: the subject must be visible AND make opening
irresistible. Shareability is near-zero — emails are rarely forwarded, the goal is the
open, not the share. Pacing matters only as a function of the tight char budget.)

## Length / structure
- Target 4–7 words, ≤40 chars where possible.
- One idea. No subject that needs the preview text to make sense (though the preview
  text can extend/complete it).
- Lowercase or sentence case often outperforms Title Case (feels personal, not broadcast).
- Personal/curiosity-driven > announcement-style. Avoid "Newsletter #42" energy.

## Types that over-index
Curiosity/Secret-Reveal, Question, Exact Pain, Personal Story (very short form).
List Promise works if the number is small and the char budget allows.

## Worked examples
Topic: re-engaging a cold lead.
"did I lose you?"  (15 chars)
D1 10 · D2 9 · D3 10 · D4 2 → 9.20/10. Pattern-interrupt, personal, opens the loop, full in fold.

"the 3-line email that closed $12k"  (33 chars)
D1 9 · D2 9 · D3 8 · D4 4 → 8.60/10. Number + outcome + withheld mechanism, inside fold.
